About this archive

How each picture is dated

A picture's date is the earliest believable date found for it, from these sources:

  • Inside the file: a date stored in the image itself.
  • In the address: a date in the picture's original web address, such as a folder named 010617.
  • Server: the original web server's last-modified date, usually the day it was uploaded.
  • Forum: the day it was first linked on a forum.
  • First archived: the day the Wayback Machine first saved it.
  • Listed: the day the Wayback Machine saved a page that already showed it, such as a gallery page.

These dates mean "the picture existed by this day". Many were taken earlier.

Some pictures are dated more closely by estimate instead, and say so:

  • Level: the character's level shows in the picture, and the character's level history on auno.org says when it was at that level.
  • File order: the player numbered their screenshots in order, so an undated one sits between its dated neighbours.
  • Series: the date of the other pictures in the same series.
  • Copy: the earliest date of another copy of the same picture.

How the pictures were found

Every picture here is a copy the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine saved, and each one links back to it. Finding them meant following old links from one site to the next:

  1. JeuxOnLine's French forum. JeuxOnLine still keeps its old forums online as a read-only archive, including its Anarchy Online forum. Its threads from 2001 to 2003 were read for links to pictures and to the sites that hosted them, which is how anarchylore.com and many other fan sites turned up.
  2. Fan-site directories. AO-Basher's lists of guild and fan sites pointed to about 200 more community sites, each checked for saved pictures.
  3. The big sites. Funcom's own anarchy-online.com, AO Stratics and IGN's AO Vault: screenshot galleries, "screenshot of the day" features, screenshot contests and news pictures.
  4. The orgs. The websites of the largest Atlantean Omni-Tek orgs, found through auno.org's org rankings from 2002 and 2003 and Funcom's org pages.
  5. Funcom's forums, post by post. Funcom's forum archive went offline in 2025, shortly after the Wayback Machine saved most of it. Every saved thread from 2001 to 2003, about 25,000 of them, was read for the pictures players showed in their posts, the fan and org sites they pointed to, and other pictures kept in the same folders. Many of those pictures were saved only years later, while still online, and are dated by the day they were uploaded or first linked. The threads themselves can be read in the forum archive, rebuilt the way the forum looked in 2002.
  6. Player pages. Personal galleries found along the way, such as MiaJong's long-running screenshot log.

Only what the Wayback Machine happened to save could be recovered. Most pictures linked in forum posts, especially on personal web space, were never archived. The search covered 1999 to 2003.

More concept art

This archive focuses on players' screenshots and keeps only a little concept art. For far more of it, see Timba's concept art collection, a much larger and more carefully curated archive of Anarchy Online's art.
